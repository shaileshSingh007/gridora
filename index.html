How to play

Tap ROTATE to turn clockwise, then MOVE to roll forward until you hit something.

The glowing ring on the board shows exactly where you will stop.

Avoid the spiky cactus balls - touch one and you burst apart. Collect every yellow star to reveal the gate, then roll through it.

Grab stars back-to-back for combo bonus points. Use ↻ to restart a level.

VIEW switches between hero and top-down cameras. Keyboard: arrows or WASD, Space to move, C for view.
