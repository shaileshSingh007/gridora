How to Play

Tap ROTATE to turn your hero clockwise.

Tap MOVE to slide forward in the direction your hero faces.

Collect every power cell to unlock the gate, then reach the glowing gate on the right edge.

Use the VIEW button to toggle between the hero camera and the top-down board camera.
